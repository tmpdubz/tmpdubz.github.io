Selected work at Shopify

From recurring incidents to lasting fixes.

Two systems I built after analysing patterns across months of production incidents.

Capacity planning · Ruby · GKE

Database capacity that follows demand

Manual capacity changes could leave databases oversized or short of capacity. I built automation that governs database sizing across Shopify’s entire storefront fleet.

It uses a demand model from data science to maintain seasonal baselines, adds capacity for flash sales, and scales back afterward.

Overload protection · Ruby · MySQL

Protecting checkouts when databases are overloaded

I built an adaptive rate limiter integrated across Shopify’s Admin GraphQL API. When databases are under sustained pressure, it slows API traffic to protect checkouts and storefronts.

Responders get time to identify the offending workloads and address the problem.
